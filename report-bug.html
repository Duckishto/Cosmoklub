Report a Bug

Found something broken, glitchy, or just plain wrong? Tell us what happened below and we'll take a look.

What makes a great report

The more detail you give us, the faster we can track down and fix the issue.

Clear steps

What you clicked, typed, or did right before it happened.

Expected vs. actual

What you thought would happen, and what happened instead.

Device & browser

Some bugs only show up on certain browsers or screen sizes.

Screenshot, if you can

A picture (or a link to one) is worth a thousand words.

Severity guide

  • Minor — a small visual glitch, doesn't block anything.
  • Moderate — annoying, but there's a workaround.
  • Major — a core feature doesn't work.
  • Critical — the site is broken or unusable.
